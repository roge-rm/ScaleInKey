scaleinkey_default.sf2

Derived from "FluidR3 GM" by Frank Wen (MIT License), trimmed to just the
Acoustic Grand Piano, Nylon String Guitar, Acoustic Bass, and Banjo
presets/samples (the timbres ScaleInKey needs for its Piano/Guitar/
Ukulele/Bass diagrams — Ukulele uses the Banjo patch, since General MIDI
has no dedicated ukulele instrument and Banjo's bright, short-decay
plucked character is a closer substitute than reusing the nylon guitar
patch). All other instruments, drum kits, and metadata from the original
151MB soundfont were removed to keep the app's bundled asset small;
sample audio data, generators, and envelopes for the kept presets are
otherwise unmodified (stereo left/right sample links were flattened to
independent mono samples for simplicity).
